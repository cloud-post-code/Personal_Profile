Massachusetts AI Skills Guide Initiative

SMB Workforce Development Discovery Session

Small-group working sessions with the people who support small businesses every day. No pitch, no product demo — your experience is the agenda.

As part of the Massachusetts AI Skills Guide Initiative, we are convening an SMB Workforce Development Discovery Session with people who work closely with small businesses — trainers, chamber and SBDC staff, workforce development leaders, engineers, and other ecosystem partners.

AI is evolving rapidly, but small businesses are adopting it at very different speeds. To build effective workforce programs, training, and support, we need a clearer understanding of what business owners are actually experiencing — and that understanding can only come from collaboration across the people who support them every day.

Together, we will explore

  • Which businesses are actively adopting AI and which are being left behind
  • Where owners become stuck or abandon AI tools
  • The fears, objections, and practical barriers owners raise
  • The AI tools and use cases gaining traction
  • The industries and business communities showing the greatest readiness
  • The training, support, and resources that successfully move owners forward
  • The unmet needs that current solutions do not adequately address

We will also identify owners and community partners who may be interested in participating in future sessions. We would be honored to have your perspective and experience as part of this collaborative discussion.
